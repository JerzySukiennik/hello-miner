Processing (ffmpeg, see Niepotrzebne/audio-src/build.sh)
- one-shots: silence trimmed at both ends, downmixed to mono, peak-normalised
  to -1 dBFS, encoded MP3 96 kbps / 44.1 kHz.
- ambient.mp3 is not a raw Kenney file: a 4 s steady section of
  spaceEngineLow_000.ogg was low-pass filtered (900 Hz), slowed to 0.85x and
  crossfade-chained into a 20 s loop whose end crossfades back into its start,
  then peak-normalised, widened to stereo and encoded MP3 96 kbps.
  It remains CC0 material; the derivative is likewise CC0.
