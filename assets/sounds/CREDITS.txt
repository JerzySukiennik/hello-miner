Processing (ffmpeg, see Niepotrzebne/audio-src/build.sh)
- one-shots: silence trimmed at both ends, downmixed to mono, peak-normalised
  to -1 dBFS, encoded MP3 96 kbps / 44.1 kHz.
- ambient.mp3 is not a raw Kenney file: a 4 s steady section of
  spaceEngineLow_000.ogg was low-pass filtered (900 Hz), slowed to 0.85x and
  crossfade-chained into a 20 s loop whose end crossfades back into its start,
  then peak-normalised, widened to stereo and encoded MP3 96 kbps.
  It remains CC0 material; the derivative is likewise CC0.

--- Round 2: richer mining and drone sounds (2026-09-06) ---
Rebuilt by Niepotrzebne/audio-src/build2.sh from the same Kenney CC0 packs.

mine.mp3 / mine1.mp3 / mine2.mp3
  impactMining_000/001/002 (Kenney Impact Sounds, CC0) layered with
  impactGeneric_light_000/001/002 delayed 70 ms at 42% gain, so each strike
  lands and then throws debris. One of the three is picked at random per hit.

crack.mp3
  impactMining_003 mixed with impactPlate_heavy_000 at 55% gain — the heavier
  hit used when the drone is breaking a boulder rather than ore.

rotors.mp3
  spaceEngineSmall_000 (Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds, CC0): a 3 s section low-passed
  at 1400 Hz, high-passed at 90 Hz, slowed to 0.92x and crossfaded onto itself
  for a seamless 6 s loop. Played continuously at low gain; the gain follows
  how many drones are mid-action, so the mine hums when it is working.

All processing: ffmpeg, loudnorm I=-16 TP=-1.0, mono, MP3 q6.
